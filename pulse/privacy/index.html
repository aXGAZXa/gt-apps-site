Pulse is a music rhythm game for iPhone and Android, made by GT Apps Ltd. This policy explains what happens to information about you when you play it.

The short version

  • Your scores, stars, settings and progress stay on your phone. We never see them.
  • Pulse has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking of its own.
  • Pulse never asks you to sign in to anything. If your phone is already signed in to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, Apple or Google sign you in to Pulse automatically and handle that data under their own privacy policies. We only use it to show achievements and leaderboards in the game.
  • Apple and Google handle every purchase. We never see your card or payment details.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

2. What stays on your device

Pulse saves the following on your phone, in the app’s own private storage, so the game can remember where you are:

  • your best score and star rating for each song and difficulty;
  • how many times you’ve played each song, and the date you last played it;
  • your settings, including your audio calibration (the timing offset you set so taps match the music; Pulse may keep a separate offset for different kinds of audio output, such as Bluetooth headphones);
  • that you’ve accepted the health and safety notice, and which version of it you accepted;
  • whether you’ve been offered the tutorial;
  • which songs and packs you own, as confirmed by the App Store or Google Play.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by Pulse. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it if it’s lost. Deleting Pulse deletes it. Your phone’s own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may include it; that backup is controlled by you and by Apple or Google, not by us.

Saving this information on your device is strictly necessary for the game to work as you’d expect, so we don’t ask for separate consent for it.

3. What we don’t do

Pulse does not:

  • ask you to create an account with us;
  • show adverts;
  • include analytics, advertising or tracking tools of our own;
  • track you across other companies’ apps or websites;
  • use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos;
  • sell or share your personal information.

Pulse does not need an internet connection to play, apart from purchases and the optional Game Center and Google Play Games features.

4. Apple Game Center and Google Play Games (optional)

Pulse works with Apple Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android, for achievements and one leaderboard per song. You don’t need either to play: the whole game works without them, and your scores and stars are always kept on your phone as well.

How it works. Pulse never asks you to sign in. If your phone is already signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games, Apple or Google sign you in to Pulse automatically when the game starts (you may see a short welcome banner from them). If your phone isn’t signed in, Pulse leaves it that way, and the achievements and leaderboard buttons don’t appear. When you are signed in, Pulse sends your achievements and your best score for each song to Apple or Google, so they can show them in your profile and on the leaderboards. Leaderboards show your gamer name (and picture, if you have one) and score to other players, as your Game Center or Play Games settings allow.

Friends. On a song’s leaderboard you can switch on “Show friends” to see how your Game Center or Play Games friends did. Apple or Google will first ask whether you’re happy for Pulse to see your friends list. If you say no, the normal leaderboard still works. Pulse only uses your friends list to show that leaderboard while you’re looking at it; we don’t keep it, and it never reaches us.

Who is responsible. Apple and Google run these services and decide how the data in them is used, under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

On Android, Google Play Games also collects some information about how its service is working (diagnostics and usage) for its own purposes when Pulse starts, as Google describes in its policy. This is Google’s collection, not ours, and it never reaches us.

Turning it off. You can sign out of Game Center in your iPhone’s Settings, or manage and delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account. You can change who sees your profile and whether games can see your friends in the same places.

5. Purchases

Pulse’s first pack is free. Other songs and packs can be bought in the game. Every purchase is made through the App Store or Google Play, and Apple or Google process the payment under their own terms and privacy policies.

We never receive your card or payment details. Pulse only receives confirmation of what you own, so it can unlock it; “Restore purchases” asks the store for the same confirmation again.

Apple and Google send us sales reports and handle refunds. These are mostly totals, though they may include limited details of individual orders (such as an order number, the product, the price and the country) so that we can deal with refunds, tax and accounts. We use these only for those purposes.

6. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to help with your question.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you. We keep your emails for up to two years after our last exchange with you, then delete them, unless we need to keep something longer for a legal reason (for example, a refund dispute).

7. Information Apple and Google give us about the app

Apple and Google give app makers statistics about their apps, such as downloads, sales and countries, and crash reports from people who have chosen in their phone’s settings to share them with app developers. These come to us in a form that doesn’t tell us who you are. We use them only to understand how Pulse is doing and to fix problems.

9. Children

Pulse is a game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. Its age ratings are 13+ on the App Store, 12+ on Google Play and PEGI 12.

Pulse doesn’t ask anyone for personal information, and it has no accounts, adverts, chat or tracking. We don’t collect personal information through the game from anyone, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age where they live) in any other way. Leaderboards and friends only appear if the phone is already signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games. Parents can manage these, and who can see a child’s profile, with Screen Time and Family Sharing on iPhone, or Google Family Link on Android.

Parents and guardians. If you have a question about your child’s use of Pulse, or believe your child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us at [privacy email]. We will delete it.
