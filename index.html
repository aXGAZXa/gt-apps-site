About

A one-person studio

GT-Apps is a solo developer with a lifelong passion for mobile games and gaming. Every game and app is designed, built and supported in-house.

The aim is simple: a small number of carefully made things, released when they are ready, that are a pleasure to pick up and easy to come back to.
