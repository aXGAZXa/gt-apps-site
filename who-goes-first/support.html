Common questions

How do I add or remove players?

Tap the people icon at the top of the spinner. Use Add player to add someone, the pencil to edit them, or swipe a player to the left to remove them.

Guests came round — how do I include them just for tonight?

Add them once, then tap their name on the Players screen to switch them on or off. Everyone stays in your list, and the app remembers who is switched on, so your usual players are ready next time.

Is the spinner random?

Yes. The winner is picked with your phone’s secure random number generator before the wheel even starts turning; the wheel then lands on them. Every active player has exactly the same chance.

How do I change or re-centre a player’s photo?

Players → pencil → tap the photo. You can take a new one, choose from your library, Adjust crop, or remove it.

I paid to remove ads but I’m seeing them again

Go to Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. On iPhone, family members included in your Family Sharing group get it too — they just tap Restore purchases.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Settings → Privacy choices (shown in the UK and EU). See the privacy policy for details.

Will I lose my players if I change phone?

Players are stored only on your phone. They move to a new phone if you restore it from an iCloud or Google backup; otherwise you’ll need to add them again.
