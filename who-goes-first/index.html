Every board game starts with the same argument. Who Goes First? ends it: tap the screen, the colour wheel spins, and the winner pops up with their photo — “Sam is going first!”

  • Tap anywhere to spin — a big, bright colour wheel with a satisfying slow-down.
  • Your regulars, remembered — add the family once; they’re ready every game night.
  • Guests in a tap — add friends who come round and switch them on or off; the app remembers who’s playing.
  • Player photos — optional, cropped to a circle, shown when they win.
  • Genuinely fair — every player has exactly the same chance.
  • Private — names and photos never leave your phone. No accounts.
Free with a small banner ad. A one-off “Remove ads” purchase turns them off for good — and on iPhone it’s shared with your whole family.
